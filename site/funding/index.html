Grants

This project fits the scope of a few real, public funding programs. See the Grant Pitch page for the full one-page case (also mirrored at docs/GRANT_PITCH.md), or reach out directly — codesbyfebin@gmail.com.

Ethereum Foundation ESP

Funds core infra and zk tooling. Relevant here: a from-scratch AIR with a documented no-secret-witness soundness argument, and an on-chain verifier whose trust model is written down rather than glossed over (THREAT_MODEL.md).

Protocol Labs Research

Verifiable-compute research is directly in scope. The honest gap analysis in RECURSION.md — what real proof recursion would take, not a claim that it's already done — is exactly the kind of scoping this kind of program tends to want to see.

Gitcoin Grants

Public-goods, quadratic-funding round for open source. A small, testable, honestly-documented zkVM is a reasonable fit for a round aimed at public infrastructure rather than products.
